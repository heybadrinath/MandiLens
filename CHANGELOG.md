# Changelog

Notable user-facing changes to MandiLens are recorded here. Release tags follow semantic
versioning while the product is under active development.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-07-20

### Added

- Prepared market intelligence for six South Indian states, 120 markets, and 14 commodities.
- A searchable Market Explorer with freshness, reporting consistency, and source context.
- Common-date market comparison with explicit quantity and cost assumptions.
- Lead-specific seven-day outlooks with uncertainty ranges and reliability evidence.
- English plus nine Indian-language interfaces with responsive navigation.
- Detailed methodology, sources, limitations, data-quality, reliability, and architecture reports.
- Contribution guidance, a pull-request template, and current production screenshots.

### Changed

- Reworked charts, tables, filters, comparison steps, and technical pages for clearer reading.
- Separated observed reports, forecast estimates, and user-entered assumptions throughout the UI.
- Expanded the batch pipeline, published artifacts, and model evaluation to the South India scope.

### Fixed

- Added lead-aware forecast movement so seven forecast dates no longer repeat one point estimate.
- Improved responsive layouts and long translated text handling across supported languages.
- Corrected repository screenshots that did not represent the current MandiLens interface.
