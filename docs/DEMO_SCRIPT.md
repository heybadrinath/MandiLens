# Short project demo script

## 90-second demo

**0:00–0:10 — Frame the problem**

“A high mandi price does not necessarily mean a better sale after cost, freshness, and uncertainty.
MandiLens turns official Maharashtra reports into a comparison, not a promise.”

**0:10–0:25 — Establish evidence**

Point to the source-through date, validated row count, covered series, and ₹0 operating-cost status.
Explain that the selected scope is onion, potato, and tomato because reliability mattered more than
nationwide appearance.

**0:25–0:45 — Explore a market**

Switch commodity and district, choose a reference market, and change the forecast horizon. Show the
observed min/modal/max lens, historical line, forecast interval area, seasonal bars, freshness,
arrivals, and missing-report count.

**0:45–1:05 — Make the decision transparent**

Enter one tonne. Add different total transport costs for two markets. Show the ranking change and
the point plus low/high net range. Read the displayed formula and mention that no distance, route,
commission, or transport rate is invented.

**1:05–1:20 — Defend the model**

Open Model Performance. Show the four equal-sample methods and four chronological folds. Explain
that the tree reduced MAE by 1.12% versus the strongest baseline—enough to clear the preset rule,
but deliberately described as a narrow win.

**1:20–1:30 — Close on reliability**

Open Data Quality or Sources. Mention 26 explicit exclusions, retained anomalies, Government Open
Data Licence attribution, weekly fail-safe refresh, static free deployment, and responsible-use
limitations.

## Representative calculation to verify during the demo

1. Select a market with a ₹2,500/quintal forecast.
2. Enter 1 tonne, which converts to 10 quintals.
3. Enter ₹2,000 total market cost.
4. Expected point estimate: `₹2,500 × 10 − ₹2,000 = ₹23,000`.
5. Change a competing market’s cost enough to reverse the rank and show that the table updates.

Use the actual visible market values during the production demo; the numbers above are only a
calculation check, not source observations or a claimed current price.
