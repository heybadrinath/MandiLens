# Responsible use

MandiLens is a decision-support and research interface. It is not a guaranteed quote, sale
instruction, trading system, or financial adviser.

## Before using a comparison

- Confirm the latest price and buying status directly with the market.
- Confirm the crop variety, grade, lot, packaging, and sale unit.
- Enter realistic market-specific transport and handling costs.
- Consider the low end of the forecast net range, not only the point estimate.
- Include commission, labour, loading, unloading, tolls, spoilage, tax, and payment timing when they
  apply.
- Consider markets outside the selected well-reported subset.

## Data limitations

- Missing source reports do not prove no trade occurred.
- The selected markets are biased toward regular recent reporting.
- Historical data can be revised after retrieval.
- Market-day aggregation can differ from a specific lot price.
- Source arrivals and varieties can be inconsistently recorded.
- Anomaly flags indicate statistical unusualness, not confirmed source errors.

## Forecast limitations

- Future shocks can fall outside an empirically calibrated interval.
- Historical interval coverage does not guarantee future coverage.
- The tree’s current improvement over the strongest baseline is small.
- Performance differs materially by commodity and forecast horizon.
- Feature importance is predictive behavior, not causation.

## Privacy

The application has no accounts, tracking, database, server-side form submission, or stored user
input. Quantity and cost values remain in browser memory until the page is closed or refreshed.
